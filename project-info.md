About the hackathon A fertility clinic runs on a lot of small, repeated jobs. Someone has to keep track of who is due for a followup, dig through old reports before a consultation, pass a case from one department to the next, edit the same forms again and again, and answer the same questions all day. None of this is hard, but it adds up, and it takes time away from patients. In this hackathon, students build working prototypes that take some of that load off clinic staff using AI tools. We want practical ideas that a real front desk or lab team could use, not just good-looking slides.

Problem 1: Patient Follow-up Management THE PROBLEM Fertility treatment needs many consultations, procedures and follow-ups. Staff track due and overdue follow-ups by hand and contact patients one by one, which adds to their workload and makes missed appointments more likely. Fertility Clinic Automation Hackathon1 WHAT YOUR SYSTEM SHOULD DO Keep track of every patient’s follow-up dates automatically, show staff what is due or overdue, and contact patients without someone having to pick up the phone. IDEAS TO GET YOU STARTED :: (Suggestions to help you start, implement own idea if you have one) • A “calls for today” list that builds itself each morning, with the most overdue cases at the top. • WhatsApp or voice reminders in the patient’s own language, where the patient can reply to confirm or ask to reschedule. • If a patient does not respond, the system tries again later and then hands the case to a staff member. • A simple view of how many follow-ups were completed, missed or rescheduled this week. WHAT TO SHOW IN THE DEMO A patient comes due for follow-up, gets a reminder, replies to reschedule, and the staff list and calendar update on their own.
